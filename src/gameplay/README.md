# Gameplay

The gameplay layer owns the shared route/collision contract and deliberately bounded sequential-enemy showcase state used by Android and Windows.

`SwordCombat.h` owns the player swing/parry action timeline, the shared player hit range/cone, skeleton locomotion/attack/stagger/death timing, deterministic attack-token ownership, and contact pulses. Swing contact resolves once on entry to the `0.18-0.34 s` active phase. Skeleton melee resolves once on entry to its `1.12-1.30 s` active phase. Rendering receives immutable player/enemy action snapshots; it never decides hits or combat phases.

`ShowcaseGameplay.h` owns player vitality/life phase, lantern failure, the smoothed articulated lower-body pose (opposed stride, pelvis bob/sway, torso counter-twist, knee bend, foot lift, and toe roll), route lighting, the plural roster/selected-enemy director, lich range/charge/hit/death/roof/dawn/ending state, and shared snapshots. `SpatialAudio.h` owns stereo pan, distance rolloff, route obstruction, and footstep cadence. `ShowcaseCheckpoints.h` and `ShowcaseReplay.h` provide thirteen deterministic Debug presets and a 13-waypoint collision-integrated route replay; `two-enemy-combat` is the explicit pair proof, `finale-roof` intentionally stops just before the completed ending overlay, and release-safe encounter retry remains limited to `opening` (skeleton pair) and `mirror` (lich).

`simulation/GameSimulation.cpp` is now the cross-platform gameplay authority that composes those bounded authored systems at a fixed 60 Hz. `InputSnapshot`/`InputMailbox` provide coherent continuous input plus independent monotonic swing/parry/reset/retry commands, `SimulationSnapshot` is the immutable renderer/UI view, and the fixed-capacity `GameplayEvent` queue is the semantic audio/haptics boundary. Platform code must not recreate this orchestration.

Accepted nonfatal player hits emit `PlayerDamaged`; the lethal hit emits only `PlayerKilled`, so platform feedback does not layer damage and fatal haptics for the same hit. Android preserves the authored 140 ms separation between a skeleton sword impact and its subsequent fall cue.

Implemented combat includes a bounded two-skeleton opening encounter with stable identities, deterministic separation and single-attacker ownership, independent persistent deaths until retry/reset, timed frontal parry with an 800 ms stationary token-holding stagger, a three-hit ranged lich finale, and three player vitality with one-second invulnerability, a 0.65-second fatal hold, and retry/restart/quit death flow. Parry does not affect the lich, provide damage, or buffer while unavailable. Lich sword damage now uses the same visible active-window range/cone test while retaining its two-second accepted-hit lockout. Benchmark, replay, and capture paths are damage-immune. Held guard, dodge, broader AI, and more than two simultaneous skinned enemies remain deferred.
