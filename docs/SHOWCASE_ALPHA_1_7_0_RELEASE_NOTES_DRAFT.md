# Horde Lantern RT 1.7.0 release notes

Package version: `1.7.0`
Android version code: `11`

## A fuller journey

- Denser forest surroundings, natural route boundaries, selective fencing, chimney smoke and a view toward Bellwether. The demo ends before the unfinished village.
- Staged combat teaching, skeleton revival into live combat, enemy attack warnings and directional hit feedback.
- A healing flask with readable charges and earned refill basins. Kit guides you to the upper-rope basin and responds to your remaining flask charges.
- Three save slots, encounter Retry, return to the main menu and hold Attack to sheathe your sword.
- Resolution settings from 25% to 100%, with retained personal graphics choices.

## Benchmarks and sharing

Standard uses a fixed graphics preset and viewport for repeatable comparisons, then restores your saved settings. Custom uses your selected graphics settings in the scripted benchmark route. Neither is a promise of frame rate throughout ordinary gameplay.

Results can be shared privately with the developer or, with separate explicit consent, published on the community results website. Public results are unverified and unranked.

Community results: https://samfa12.com/games/the-horde/benchmark-stats/

## Compatibility

Native Vulkan hardware ray tracing is required on Windows and Android. There is no raster or software-rendering fallback. Performance depends on hardware, graphics settings and scene workload; no sustained mobile frame-rate guarantee is made.
