**Experimental and unadmitted.** The owner approved a lower-detail world body,
keeping first-person arms unchanged, followed by an independent Kit study. This
checkpoint prepares one existing-asset derivative and verifies native CPU import,
shared pose and one short desktop CPU comparison. Production assets/runtime,
worker OFF default, shaders, simulation and saved settings are unchanged.
No RT image, frame-time, phone or shipping admission follows from these results.

**Quality concern found by independent static inspection:** gauntlet triangles
whose winding opposes their average vertex normal increased0→2, and the body
remainder12→13. BodyPrimary also gained a roughly1.62×10⁻¹¹ m² sliver. These
are retained concerns, not demonstrated phone/render faults or a native-gate
pass. No correction/second simplification was made. Resolve or reject them
before any promotion; the CPU saving does not override them.
