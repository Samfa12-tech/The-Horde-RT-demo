# Compact torch carry: proposed experience tradeoff

Proposal only, based on the retained [endpoint rejection](../evidence/2026-10-24-compact-carry-study/README.md)
and [joint-pose rejection](../evidence/2026-10-23-torch-joint-pose-withdrawn/README.md).
No requirement, test, pose, asset, camera, collider, light or production binding
changes here. The phone recording's dark-world cause remains unverified.

The demonstrated problem has two competing requirements. At tight wall/ceiling
angles the original long Rag torch, its complete animated fire envelope, the
Grip and the shadow endpoints must remain in the room. Pulling that unchanged
assembly close enough to the eye to keep both hand and head framed can make the
wrapped head dominate the picture. Moving it down/aside can recover containment
while taking the offhand out of the frame. The tested cross-chest endpoint still
misses complete flame containment by 72.6 mm at one witness, so it does not even
establish a successful compromise. It must remain rejected.

There is no proof that every possible pose is infeasible. However, another
mirrored cross-chest variation or a general solver is not justified by the
available evidence. Before another family, select the experience to preserve:

1. **Recommended for a bounded proof: preserve the original physical assembly,
   camera and player collision, but permit the offhand/torch head to leave the
   primary frame temporarily at extreme clearance.** Author a tucked carry with
   a smooth entry/exit and stable held intent; keep full mesh/fire containment,
   lighting, Grip and all secondary visibility. Restore the normal visible carry
   as clearance returns. This is a proposed exception to the current framing
   requirement, not permission to call an existing zero-arm witness a pass or
   disable its test. Exact permitted witnesses and visible transition must be
   reviewed before adoption; arbitrary missing arms remain a defect.
2. **If continuous visible hand/head is required:** approve a distinct compact
   carry geometry study with a measured original-identity silhouette. Preserve
   originals and do not assume shortening the handle, shrinking the torch or
   changing fire size automatically solves the full envelope. Any altered asset
   needs independent provenance, readable native views, physical containment and
   shadow/reflection/light evidence before replacing production geometry.

Increasing player stand-off could restrict narrow passages and is not recommended
as the first response. Camera shifts, a screen overlay, detached light and hidden
secondary geometry remain outside these choices. Neither option authorizes a
general rendering/animation rewrite or a return to repeated threshold tuning.

This is a separate future decision from the canopy/flask/effects questions already
with Sam. Do not interrupt those or silently adopt this framing exception. While
it is pending, current runtime and every original failed receipt remain intact.

Audio/haptic manual revalidation required for this proposal: **NO**. A later
accepted physical carry can change spatial lighting but must not change audio
semantics or use this document as proof of natural gameplay acceptance.
