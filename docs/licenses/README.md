The PDF names Jason Steele as licensor and permits worldwide, non-exclusive,
perpetual royalty-free use in games and other personal or commercial media.
It prohibits claiming authorship, reselling the sounds, and use in national
government projects, law-enforcement projects, or projects produced by groups
categorized as hate groups by SPLC or CAHN. Breaching the terms terminates the
licence; the agreement states that United States law applies. The website
says credit is optional. This is a custom licence, not CC0 or the repository's
source-code licence.

The game uses selected clips and processed derivatives, with source and runtime
identities recorded in `ASSET_LICENSES.md` and the audio manifests. This record
does not change their authorship or relicense them. It does not certify the
pending in-game listening and volume acceptance.
