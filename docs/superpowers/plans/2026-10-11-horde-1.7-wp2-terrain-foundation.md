## Terrain geometry and support

The shared support/render route mesh is 24 m wide, with six cross-lanes per centerline segment and a gentle authored cross-fall. `ResolveWorldRouteSupport` computes support from those exact top triangles. The upload batch adds the matching 84 top triangles, 84 underside triangles at Y `-5`, 28 outer-bank triangles and 24 end-cap triangles. Those opaque faces close the bounded ground volume. `WorldRouteTerrainMovementClear` sweeps a capsule against actual top-mesh support. Route trunk collision and rendering also share the same AABBs through `WorldRouteBlockoutBoxes` and `WorldRouteBlockoutMovementClear`. The existing 2.4 m route ribbon is now part of this terrain.

The planned route marks and four Bellwether shells use the same translation. Bellwether remains closed, nonplayable geometry and receives no support. Existing simple opaque forest boxes remain measurement props; they do not claim final tree art or foliage quality.

The standalone laboratory has 556 triangles. The chapter excludes its initial ramp/roof/step boxes, adds a sealed start cap, and retains 1,144 combined terrain, rim, anchor, coarse tree, town and explicitly non-effect marker triangles after the rescue-journey append. This is a host geometry count, not a GPU memory, ray-cost, FPS or production budget. The existing synchronous resident/staged comparison must retain identical geometry.
