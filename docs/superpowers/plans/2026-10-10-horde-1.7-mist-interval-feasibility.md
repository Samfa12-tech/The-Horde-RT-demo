# Bounded rope–mist interval feasibility

**Outcome: defer production integration.** Common density-cell processing makes
a substantial static-size saving, but neither initial High variant satisfies all
unchanged contracts. The stronger CPU discriminator also retains three strict
per-cell failures. No budgets, production shaders/packages, ABI, AS ownership,
geometry, masks, density, extinction, quality or surface-shadow policy changed
in this experiment. No six-point reference was adopted.

## Correctness discriminator

The new executable CPU check uses the exact frozen rope, both finite aperture
positions, normalized cone planes and verified historical `current-depth-3`
readback. It compares individual clipped endpoints, union counts/positions and
projected cell coverage, rather than only total union length. Historical depth
presentation/hash/state checks are custody checks, not a new GPU test.

- Six synthetic fixtures cover source filtering, unordered overlap, disconnected
  and thin intervals, raw 64/65 and callback 512/513 boundaries. The 65th raw
  interval and 513th callback reject; neither overflow is hidden.
- Six predetermined rays × both sources yield 12 actual frozen CPU pairs, with
  nonempty witnesses for each aperture. Endpoint error is at most
  `7.36e-6 m` against the `50e-6 m` diagnostic endpoint criterion.
- Every 2/6/8 cell vector is compared. Source one, pixel `(465,286)`, fails the
  original fixed `2e-5` coverage discriminator: errors are
  `0.0007696972`, `0.0023341909` and `0.0031126037`, respectively.
- Equal-length displaced intervals are rejected by endpoint and cell-vector
  comparisons. The actual-ray displacement control also distinguishes placement.
- 288 deterministic float32 schedule comparisons preserve ordered sample
  centres, scattering and extinction exactly in the CPU arithmetic model. This
  is not a statement of GPU compiler/driver equivalence or all-light correctness.

A contextual roundoff bound explains why small endpoint differences can produce
larger coverage fractions in a short cell. It does not replace the fixed
discriminator or turn these failures into acceptance. Initial intermediate
reports were overwritten by the first runner; their available agent-reported
summary and that retention failure are disclosed in
[methodology-and-failures.json](../../evidence/2026-10-10-mist-interval-feasibility/methodology-and-failures.json).
The final strict failed report/log is retained and the runner now chooses unused
numbered outputs. GPU endpoint/cell readback remains **not run**; its explicit
probe schema is a future diagnostic design, not implemented discovery evidence.

## One static hypothesis and measured result

Only the three unrolled density branches were replaced by one common ordered
body with an exact selected count of 2, 6 or 8. The existing projected cell
coverage formula, discovery, four-plane clipping, insertion sort, overlap union,
64 raw intervals, 512 callbacks and invalid/overflow behavior were retained.
Neither union simplification nor descriptor/AS redesign was attempted.

The Vulkan SDK 1.4.350.0 tool binaries were hash-matched to the reference.
`GenericRetained` kept its five optimizer passes; `LegacyInlined` kept glslang
`-Os` and spirv-opt `-O`. Both candidates compile, optimize and pass spirv-val
for Vulkan 1.2. That is not frozen admission or physical rendering evidence.

Generic saves 9,492 bytes; Opaque saves 57,628 bytes. Generic adds one density
loop while reducing repeated calls. Opaque's fully inlined repeated density,
fire and interval scans are consolidated; its static loop count falls by 29.
These are static observations, not measured traversal, register, spill, memory
or frame-time improvements.

Generic's three disassembled initialization owners are `traceScene`,
`boundedShadowTransmittanceMask` and `discoverMistIntervals`. The cone initializer
is still a distinct site. Moving calls or consolidating cell arithmetic does not
solve it. Genuine initialization sharing with correct AS/query lifetimes, or a
demonstrated reduction elsewhere, has not been proved within this narrow pass.
Opaque still has 30 sites and therefore also fails the exact
`driverSafeFullyInlined=true` row classification, despite one function/zero calls.
No emergency compiler guard substituted for a row ceiling.

All other exact fields were checked from the measured modules. An initial
evidence scanner incorrectly matched a diagnostics variable name; reflection
shows Binding 22 is present in both. The corrected ledger retains the initial
scanner report, without recompiling or changing shader bytes.

Because both initial rows fail decisive structural contracts and the stricter
cell gate is unresolved, there is no demonstrated complete fit. The remaining
six variants and sustained Windows ablations/dynamic-AS update measurements were
**not run**. Repeated compilation or another broad rewrite would not resolve
these known blockers within the authorized stop/go pass.

## Recommendation and next physical checklist

**Defer integration; retain the experiment and its failures.** No budget decision
is justified by these measurements. Any later bounded attempt first needs a
concrete two-site Generic/29-site Opaque solution within all remaining maxima,
plus actual GPU endpoint/cell discrimination. Only then measure warmed sustained
traversal, selected-rope filtering, cone preparation/upload/AS update and full-frame
tail costs at unchanged settings. Android and QueryCompute must pass before
adoption; no stale cones or quality reduction may hide cost.

For a later exact-candidate Windows/Android physical check:

1. Capture CURRENT and HIGHER from the same frozen camera, rope and light state;
   retain the floor shadow and judge band placement, strength and continuation.
2. Move/bump the rope and vary the view/light; check continuous, correctly placed
   mist coverage, clear gaps and off-camera blockers without delayed/stale shadows.
3. Verify actual Pipeline/QueryCompute presentation, exact artifacts and lifecycle
   recovery on each supported target. Compare endpoints/cells and warmed costs;
   record owner visual feedback separately. Do not infer phone acceptance from RTX.

Commands, timings, hashes, source/module/disassembly artifacts, strict failures
and unchanged input/tool/budget identities are under
[the evidence directory](../../evidence/2026-10-10-mist-interval-feasibility/README.md).
