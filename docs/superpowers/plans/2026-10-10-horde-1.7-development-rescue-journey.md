## Implemented contract

- Actual lantern collection owns one rescue opening and anchored rope deployment. Chest unlock/open/claim ordering, collection-triggered chest spotlight removal, Keeper death/torch ordering and held lantern light remain independent.
- The shared fixed-step traversal module publishes solved rope particles, support/root height, hand targets, load/tension, gear ownership and generation. Both native adapters consume that snapshot. No camera elevator or render-owned gameplay height is used.
- The original shaft connects lower support -0.95 to upper support +2.05. Eye height remains support +1.65. The canopy/coping clearance lift is +2.28 support; it is a short authored handoff, not general jump/mantle physics.
- Ascent alternates bounded grips on the loaded rope, then separately transfers onto the actual apron and crosses the coping. Descent reverses the supported approach before gripping and lowering to the tomb. Both hands are free of equipment during traversal. The sword uses its actual animated body-stow mount; the claimed lantern uses an independent body-heading hip hinge and existing ring/body/glass/flame/light rig.
- Renderer admission requires existing real uploaded buffers, completed BLAS/TLAS initialization, selected pipeline and the development world's update scratch. Failed preparation consumes the interaction and leaves the player on the safe side. Late generation publication is rejected. Ordinary movement/combat edges are consumed during traversal, not replayed on release.
- Logical reconstruction/retry returns interrupted ascent to the lower side and interrupted descent to the exterior, preserving ownership. Reset clears the deployment. Durable saves, process-death recovery and campaign slots are not implemented.
- Rope vertices come from solved particles with fixed topology. Renderer-owned in-place world BLAS updates join the observed dynamic producer batch and its single BLAS-to-TLAS dependency. Existing frame fencing and resource destruction own retirement. No camera-frustum relevance culling is introduced.
- Campaign continuation suppresses the legacy dawn/ending overlay only in this development mode. The wall grate, closed entry skylight, waterfall breach, barred waterfall skylight and rescue aperture remain distinct.
