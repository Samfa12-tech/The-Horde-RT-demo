# Separate diffuse-direction bounce and texture-LOD decisions

These are bounded feasibility decisions after retaining the [selective-opacity NO-GO](ENGINEERING_1_6_1_PRIMARY_OPACITY_2026-10-01.md). Neither experiment changes production source, materials, settings, defaults or assets. Source inspection is not a performance measurement. Keep each decision separate from the mist and spatial investigations; no speculative changes are combined.

## Diffuse-direction transport: authorized optional Lower candidate, Current remains default

**Owner clarification, 5 October:** prepare this as a separate opt-in Lower indirect-lighting candidate after the current mist/device slice reaches a safe boundary. Preserve Current as default. This resolves the quality-tradeoff decision only. Preserve selected reflective/metallic receivers, mirrors, wet cobbles/puddles, glass, every water mode and held-item/player/fire reflection ownership. Gate eligible opaque receivers' secondary-ray launch rather than removing reflected objects. Matched visual/performance comparison remains required; no global reflection equality or speedup is assumed. Broader architecture work must be reported before expanding scope. The earlier defer-adoption decision below describes the unmeasured state and remains a shipping gate, while candidate preparation is now authorized. Texture LOD remains independent and unbenchmarked/deferred.


The shared opaque-primary path traces one secondary ray in Authored/Max. Reflection-selected receivers use the reflection direction and retain player/fire ownership; other opaque receivers use a deterministic diffuse direction. Ordinary secondary material decode/texture work occurs even when that branch has no additional shadow query.

One bounded candidate would retain every reflection-selected receiver, all metallic receivers, wet cobble and transmissive fallback, while skipping the deterministic diffuse-direction trace for the remaining opaque receivers. It would preserve the primary hardware query, direct/specular light, physical shadows and separate glass/water transport. It does not remove the shared query site for protected receivers; eligible pixel fraction and driver saving are unknown.

The existing shared bounce supplies both `base * bounce * (0.10 + reflective * 0.16)` and `bounce * reflective * reflectionGain`. Consequently skipping its ray removes a small reflectivity-weighted term as well as diffuse radiance on excluded receivers. Current world/prop material reflectivity is generally nonzero. Removing only the additive diffuse expression would leave the required secondary trace and has no demonstrated traversal saving. Switching the whole workload to Lean would additionally remove protected reflections and is rejected.

**Decision: defer this quality-tradeoff experiment; retain the accepted current transport.** There is no exact candidate package, native appearance review or matched Shipping timing for this proposal. Do not describe it as a lossless optimization, a completed performance test or a speedup. Reopen only as an explicitly labelled indirect-light quality choice with preserved metal/wet/glass/water/held-body reflections, isolated matched images, balanced same-setting Shipping measurements and owner acceptance. Outside source-audit SHA256 `71e957dc7aa993bbbcf8cd8f80f82c2b09befbe18364bf9b142cb27a0d8a22f8` preserves the mechanical proposal and admission cells.

## Footprint-aware texture LOD: defer the complete correction

The existing outside audit reconstructed four actual published Shipping/Mobile Pipeline/Compute OpaqueFast/Generic payloads and inspected their SPIR-V. World/prop/Lich material samples used explicit literal LOD0; environment sampling already used computed LOD. Opaque60 versus Generic10 static material sites reflects inlining, not dynamic sampling, bytes, cache misses or a measured bottleneck. That audit identifies its payloads and moving-source dependency mismatches explicitly; it is not freshness admission for the current candidate. Sampling receipt SHA256 `0c8a285f9be0e8df84474cfe0a61dda90825076c889863e051bcdb6573bfbd39` remains historical.

Actual resource headers show512×512 five-layer world diffuse/normal/ARM arrays with **one mip**,2048×2048 Lich base/emissive maps with **one mip**, and1024×1024 production prop arrays/emissive with11 levels. A shader LOD expression cannot select absent world/Lich levels. Generating those levels requires colour-correct/normal-renormalized asset production, exact provenance/hashes and measured storage/packaging, while preserving original top-level pixels.

The shared material decoder runs before the hit returns. Its local segment distance is not the complete camera-to-surface footprint after reflection, refraction, water exit or dielectric continuation. A distance-only bias would silently give the wrong ownership on secondary paths. A proper bounded proposal needs actual internal projection, transformed/current deformed triangle UV gradients, finite/grazing/degenerate policies and propagation across all nine trace call sites. Mapped/procedural normals, rough-direction discontinuities and curved water exits complicate that local estimate; additional normal/ALU work may offset locality gains.

**Decision: defer complete shared footprint/LOD admission.** No mip assets, runtime approximation, shader candidate or measured bandwidth saving is adopted. A prop-primary-only prototype could validate limited math, but cannot close world/Lich/secondary acceptance and must not become a silently partial default. Reopen with an agreed bounded propagation model, reproducible world/Lich mip chains, independent planar/deformed/secondary math checks, actual compiled sample operands, moving minification/normal/emissive review and useful matched device benefit. Geometry LOD, detail normals and parallax retain their separate [documented deferrals](ENGINEERING_1_6_2_TEMPORAL_DECISIONS.md).
