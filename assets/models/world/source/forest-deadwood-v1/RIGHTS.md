# Prepared deadwood rights and custody

`source-recovery.json` records byte-identical recovery of the supplied compact
stump and dead-snag archives. All39 payloads, editable sources, original notices,
maps and receipts are preserved. No master/backup was overwritten.

The stump was original text-only Meshy generation on the owner's paid plan.
The owner confirmed public Horde repository/runtime redistribution; that later
decision supersedes the historical preparation-only rights note in the unchanged
archive. Generated-output ownership is not a blanket CC0/MIT assertion. The
compact editable source is the repaired low-poly master; the original high-poly
Meshy package remains a separate Library source. No new generation was performed.

Dead-snag geometry derives from Quaternius `DeadTree_1` under CC0. Its exact
notice is preserved in `original-snag/source/QUATERNIUS_LICENSE.txt`; the supplied
bark maps and preparation scripts have their original provenance in that package.
The coarse LOD2 is selected without further decimation.
The [official nature-pack page](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatestylizednature.html)
was rechecked on12 October2026 and declares CC0 for that pack. This resolves the
archived notice's mismatched pack heading without editing its original bytes.

Fallen-log/root-tangle geometry comes from the already admitted original supplied
forest-dressing archive at `../forest-understory-v1/original-package/`. Its
original-authorship statement, Blender master, maps and hash manifest remain
there. No private Briarhold source was read/copied for this recovery.

`candidates/` contains separately hashed Blender tangent derivatives. Per-file
receipts prove retained geometry, winding, UV0, normals and embedded image pixels.
Native importer passes are engineering intake evidence, not automatic runtime
or phone/performance admission. Runtime selections must retain these notices and
record any material reuse separately. Editable sources stay outside packages.
