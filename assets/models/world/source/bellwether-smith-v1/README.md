# Bellwether smith yard

The exterior B06 work yard sits against the original smith building's west wall. An open timber canopy protects a raised stone forge, anvil, workbench and quench tub. The stone hardstand follows the surveyed grade. The opening toward the square and the original west door approach stay clear.

The editable Blender master retains the roof courses, pegged frame, masonry, hollow tub and individual tools. The export appends 2,464 solid and 420 walkable triangles to the town GLB. The shared town materials provide the roof clay, timber, masonry and dark fittings. A mounted wick lights the open bay through the shared RT fire system. The forge is staged cold and the artificer continues to offer the breathing rig at the mill.

Run [the authoring recipe](../../../../../tools/author-bellwether-smith.py) in Blender 5.2 with the repository root, the pinned town GLB and its adjacent manifest from `1789b134`, and a fresh output directory. The [receipt](authoring-receipt.json) pins the terrain, parent town, editable master, generated layout and layered runtime mesh.
