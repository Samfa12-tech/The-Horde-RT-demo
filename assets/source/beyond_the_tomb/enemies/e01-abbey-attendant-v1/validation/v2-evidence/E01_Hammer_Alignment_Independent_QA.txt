E01 Abbey Attendant: independent hammer-alignment QA

RESULT: PASS for the tested final asset revision.

Change verified
- RightGrip reverses 180 degrees about local X, so the mallet head exits the thumb side of the grip. Its attachment origin and mallet geometry are preserved.
- Dead alone adjusts RightHand local-Y rotation by a smooth +20-degree ramp relative to the delivered source, retaining 25 degrees of the original corrective wrist roll. The ramp runs from 20% to 55% of the clip.
- Across core, all-clips, and source-extras GLBs, the only changed binary bytes belong to those approved rotation channels. Geometry, indices, skin weights, inverse-bind data, textures, and all other animation channels remain unchanged. The separate mallet GLB is byte-identical.

Clearance and grip
- Fresh Blender imports: 576 poses at 60 Hz across Idle, Walking, Attack, and Dead.
- Additional final Dead scan: 357 poses at 120 Hz.
- Every sampled pose maintains gripping-hand contact. No mallet-head/body or other non-hand body triangle intersections were detected.
- Minimum floor clearance: Idle 731.73 mm; Walking 595.92 mm; Attack 657.52 mm; Dead 38.24 mm.
- Dead's closest sampled mallet-head vertex to a non-hand body surface is 10.32 mm, adjacent to the character's head at frame 175 of 178. This is a vertex-to-surface distance, not an exact whole-surface separation guarantee.

Reader and editable-source agreement
- Repository native CPU skin reader passes all four core clips at 60 Hz, including finite transforms and the rigid held-item socket validator.
- Native hand-relative socket checks at 20 poses confirm the expected local reversal and retained attachment position within floating-point tolerance.
- Editable Blender source and exported core GLB agree across 22 poses. Maximum exported-body vertex distance to editable-body vertices: 0.01382 mm. Maximum socket/assembled-mallet world-matrix element difference: 4.42e-6.

Scope limits
- Discrete Blender triangle-crossing checks do not prove continuous-time collision freedom or exclude every enclosed-volume overlap.
- Native GPU skinning, ray-traced presentation, gameplay, and device performance were not run by this QA pass.
