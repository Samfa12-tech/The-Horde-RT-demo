# Visual and structural review, 2026-10-06

Inspection used all eleven freshly imported GLB studio views plus the two separate source-topology overlays. The reviewer was an assistant, not the project owner or an engine admission authority.

- Skull/jaw close-up and side: eye, nasal and temporal openings read as genuine missing geometry. Teeth remain separate source islands. Selective one-level cranium/jaw smoothing improves close silhouette without globally subdividing every tooth or the whole skeleton. Matte material remains uniform; no dirt, texture, painted shadow or gore is present.
- Skull/jaw cage: silhouette remains recognizably the same source design, but forehead, brow, nose and jaw are visibly more angular. Source cage is about 8% wider and 3% taller than the smoothed master. This is a separate coarse alternative requiring gameplay-distance evaluation, not certified seamless LOD or close-up equivalent.
- Femur and humerus: recognizable long-bone silhouettes, real anatomical island selection, plausible normalized adult scale, closed manifold topology, no degenerate triangles. One subdivision level improves source-cage facets; no broader remeshing is hidden.
- Both sparse arrangements: three linked master meshes each, no support/niche geometry, collision or accepted placement implied. Source-instance checks confirm shared mesh data, origin support at Z=0 and zero inter-object triangle intersections. The final fresh-import views apply the recorded XYZ Euler rotations correctly; initial trial render orientations were corrected before these final files were packaged.
- Whole static skeleton: recognizable articulated anatomy retained from the source with smooth normals but no global subdivision. Front/back studio views contain no added enemy equipment or mechanical shell. Coarse joint silhouettes and inherited vertebral defects remain. No rig or animation accompanies the export.
- Source-topology overlays: orange outlines show the inherited skull openings and open vertebral boundaries. Red marks multi-face vertebral edges. They are diagnostic preview curves and are absent from every output GLB. The source is not watertight. Core topology cleanup is still needed before any deliberate production rigging/collision decision.

Independent local structure checks pass for all seven GLBs: one mesh/primitive/material, finite positions and normalized normals, valid indexed triangles, no degenerate triangles, identity transforms, metres, no textures, zero skins and zero animations. Source checksum remains exact. Native Horde import, Android/Windows RT, texture/material semantics in engine, final collision/clearance and owner visual acceptance are untested.
